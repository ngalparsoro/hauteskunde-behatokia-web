Legal notice and privacy

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Responsible person

This website is run by Ion Ansa Mendizabal. Development and design: Nora Galparsoro Larraza. Contact: kontaktua@hautesbehatokia.eus.

Hauteskunde Behatokia is an informational data website. It has no advertising, sells nothing and has no user accounts.

Data and content

The election results come from official public sources; their origin, licences and download dates are on the Sources page. Candidate names are those in the official results published by those sources.

The data have been processed with care, but errors are possible. Official results should be checked at the official sources. If you spot an error, please write to: kontaktua@hautesbehatokia.eus.

No cookies

This website does not use cookies and does not profile visitors. Some preferences you choose (for example the language or keeping the left menu hidden) are stored only in your browser and are never sent to us.

Privacy

We do not collect your data: there are no forms and no sign-up. Even so, to display the website your browser sends your IP address to these services, which process it:

  • Cloudflare (hosting and visit statistics): to serve and protect the website. Statistics are produced with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies and with aggregated data. Cloudflare may process data outside the European Union, under safeguards approved by the European Commission.
  • Map imagery servers: EOX IT Services GmbH (Austria, Sentinel-2), IGN España (PNOA) and IGN France (BD ORTHO). Map tiles are loaded directly from their servers.

The legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)): providing the website and keeping it secure.

If you send us an email, we will use your address and message only to reply to you, and we will not pass them on to third parties.

To access, rectify or erase your data, or to object to its processing, write to kontaktua@hautesbehatokia.eus. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
