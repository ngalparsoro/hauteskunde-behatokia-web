About

Hauteskunde Behatokia is a tool for viewing election results across the Basque Country as a whole: Hegoalde and Iparralde on the same map, from local elections to the European Parliament, by municipality and by census section or polling station.

It is a data website: it shows results, seats and how they change over time, without opinion. All data come from official public sources, listed with their download dates on the Sources page.

What you'll find here

  • Map: results for each type of election, with a timeline; in each area, the latest elections up to that date.
  • Trend: how each municipality has voted since 2011, for each type of election.
  • Image: download the map as an image, ready to share.
  • Seats: Basque Parliament, Parliament of Navarre, General Assemblies, Spanish Congress of Deputies and town councils.
  • Basque Federal Parliament: a simulation and the game «Build your coalition government» (on the Regional map, in the seats for the whole Basque Country).
  • Blocs: Hegoalde by party; the whole Basque Country and Iparralde by political bloc.
